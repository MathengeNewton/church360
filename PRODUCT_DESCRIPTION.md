# Church360 Welfare — Product Description

**Church360 Welfare** is a digital platform built for church welfare ministries. It brings member records, family registrations, contributions, and communications together in one place—so treasury, leadership, and members work from the same accurate picture.

---

## What it does

The system lets welfare teams **record and retrieve** member and family information, manage **family registrations** and **new member additions**, and see **contributions** at both family and individual level—**monthly and annually**—with **reports** for planning and accountability. Treasury can follow **incoming and outgoing money** in the context of annual and family contributions, per member and per family.

For **church leadership**, Church360 Welfare offers a straightforward way to handle welfare data and align it with the **main church**: welfare membership is maintained here and feeds the broader church for consideration of benefits, programmes, and support—so everyone is working from one trusted source.

For **welfare members**, the platform supports **clear organisation of information**, **welfare communications** (announcements and notices), and **contribution tracking** across the year—so giving is transparent and stewardship is easier. **SMS integration** (planned) will extend that reach with reminders and updates by text, making communication within the welfare and the wider church **faster and more inclusive**.

---

## In one sentence

**Church360 Welfare connects welfare membership, giving, and communication in one system—so treasury can account with confidence, leadership can serve with clarity, and members stay informed and engaged.**

---

## Who it’s for

| Role | Value |
|------|--------|
| **Treasury** | Full member and family records; contribution breakdowns and reports; visibility of money in and out. |
| **Leadership** | Simple management of registrations and member data; welfare aligned with the main church. |
| **Members** | Organised information, welfare updates, contribution history; SMS when available. |
